# M3 task plan: Design shell

**Goal**: Put the whole owner-facing admin into the new v2 shell on live KPI services: tokens, layout, role menus, command palette, dark mode, Dashboard, Orders, Customers, Reports (Overview, Sales, Delivery, Team) and Settings. Checkout forms, the public checkout and online payments are not here: they are milestone M3b (`docs/plan/M3b.md`), which starts after M3 is merged.

**Done when** (PRD §10, made testable). The Dashboard line is the milestone gate. The other lines list what else ships in this PR.
1. With the M1 fixture loaded, the owner Dashboard (`/`) shows in the new shell: North Star GH₵1.38M of GH₵1,800,000; October GH₵92k of GH₵180,000; confirmation rate 71%; delivery success rate 48% with "Target 60% · yield 34%"; cash still with agents GH₵23.4k; projected October GH₵167.8k; orders needed a day 56. Every number comes from `GET /api/dashboard/owner`. The frontend does no KPI arithmetic (grep test).
2. Dashboard budget: `GET /api/dashboard/owner` answers in under 1 second on the fixture (recorded in the PR; budget is a GUESS from PRD §7.7).
3. Every page in this milestone renders in light and dark mode at 1440 px and 760 px with no horizontal page scroll (Playwright screenshots in the PR).
4. Menu by role works: finance clerk lands on Finance; stock keeper lands on Products (Inventory comes in M4); manager sees the Dashboard with permission-based blocks hidden; a direct URL to a page the role may not open shows the "no access" page.
5. Orders: tab counts equal the fixture (All 1,445; pending 64; confirmed + preparing + ready 100; out 148; delivered 376; failed 354; returned 48; cancelled 355). Bulk status returns a per-order result report. Bulk assign works for 200 orders. Import has a check step. Export is not capped at 500 rows.
6. Customers list, detail, add, edit, delete work; a call rep sees customers they created.
7. Reports tabs Overview, Sales, Delivery and Team show the same revenue and rates as the Dashboard (test). CSV and Excel download directly; there is no PDF item.
8. Settings: profile save, photo, change password (all roles), sessions list with sign out elsewhere, business details, order statuses (read-only, 12 values), Billing with live plans.
9. Static checks pass in CI: no hex colour outside the token file; no `<button>` without handler; no `window.confirm`; wording guard (M1) green; no hard-coded `$` or `GHS` in components.

**Depends on**: M0, M1 (KPI services, state machine, fixture, `Target`, `FxRate`), M2 (`Role` / `RoleAssignment`, audit, registry).

**PR**: one PR for the whole milestone (PRD rule). Branch `feature/m3-design-shell` from `develop` (after M2 is merged), into `develop`. Never push to `main`. No push before owner approval. Because the milestone is large, the owner reviews at three internal gates on the same branch: G1 after M3-T41 (shell and Dashboard), G2 after M3-T50 (Orders and Customers), G3 after M3-T69 (Reports and Settings). Task IDs T01, T04, T05, T06, T18, T19 and T51 to T59 moved to M3b and are not reused, so every other ID keeps its number.

**Owner of every task**: builder, with Opus review.

Legend: XS = 2 h, S = 3-4 h, M = 5-6 h, L = 7-8 h. Run `npx prisma generate` first. Hand-migrate the test database after each new migration. Jest with `--forceExit`. Frontend visual checks use the `agent-browser` or `claude-in-chrome` tools.

Scope notes (what is not in M3 even though a screen mentions it): reminder chip and reply count (M9); team notes and messages blocks (M9, show an empty state); "Unpaid by agent" tab (M5); suggestion cards and agent cards (M11); "Top this month" (M6); rep app (M7); agent app (M10); Inventory page (M4); Checkout forms, public checkout, payment callback page and neutral webhook route (M3b); Connections page and provider card (M9); Operations tab (M7); North Star, Targets and Rewards tabs (M6).
