1Summary

The rebuild gives the owner one place to run the business. The owner cannot trust the numbers today, because four revenue definitions disagree. Logiswift pays one lump sum that nobody matches to orders. Stock has no location. Call reps and delivery agents have no clear targets, pay or screens. 2Architecture

Screens call controllers. Controllers call services. Services write to the tenant-scoped database. Everything an AI agent or a person does goes through one tool registry and one audit log.

Rules the build follows

  • Controllers only call services.
  • One KPI service per area is the only place that computes a number. The Dashboard, reports, chat and MCP all call it.
  • One tool registry holds every tool: name, schema, service call, permission, read or write flag.
  • Every write goes through a service that posts its own side effects (ledger, stock, history).
  • Every actor has a user row: person, AI agent, company or system.
  • Every new model has a required tenantId and is in the scoped list. New money columns are Decimal(19,4).

Stack

Frontend: React 18, Vite, TypeScript, Zustand, Recharts. Backend: Node 20, Express, Prisma 5, PostgreSQL, Redis with Bull queues, socket.io. Tests: Jest, Vitest, Playwright. The host nginx is the edge, Docker serves the built app and Cloudflare R2 holds files.

Payments sit behind a provider interface: initialize, verify, parse webhook, supported methods, fee. Paystack is the first adapter and works as today.

Order to cash Built in M3b, M4, M5, M7

From the public form to cash matched and revenue booked.

Roles
Customer, call rep, fulfilment lead, delivery partner, finance clerk
Screens
Public checkout, rep Queue, Orders, Deliveries, Finance, Dashboard
Booked
4010 revenue and 5010 cost of goods at delivery; 5070 delivery fee once; GH₵8 and 10% upsell earned
  1. The customer fills the public checkout form. The system creates the order as pending confirmation. Nothing is booked yet.
  2. The auto-confirm message goes out at once. A call rep, or the AI confirm agent, works the order from the rep Queue.
  3. The rep confirms by call. The system sets the confirmed time and saves any upsell lines. A decline or a wrong number cancels the order with a reason. If the customer is not reachable, the order stays pending and the call is logged.
  4. The fulfilment lead, or a Workflow (Region is X, Assign to), assigns a partner on Orders. With no matching Workflow the order stays unassigned. Stock moves toward the partner.
  5. The partner delivers. At delivered the system books revenue to 4010 and cost of goods to 5010, dated by delivery date. It books the delivery fee once to 5070. The rep earns GH₵8 and 10% of the upsell, but only for an order they confirmed by call. After failed or returned, only the fee from the partner's fee rule is booked.
  6. The partner keeps the fee from the cash it collects and hands in the rest. The 3PL payout or the agent deposit is matched to that net amount on Finance. A gap opens a Finding. The Dashboard shows the result.

Paid digital order Built in M3b, M5

A digital order counts as delivered revenue only when it is paid.

Roles
Customer, finance clerk
Screens
Digital checkout form, payment callback page, Orders, Finance
Booked
4020 digital revenue and 5060 payment fee on the payment day
  1. The customer opens a digital form, for example the Smart Parent's guide at GH₵59. The form offers online payment only.
  2. The form calls the payment provider interface. The system saves a pending checkout and a provider reference. The customer pays by card or bank transfer.
  3. The provider webhook arrives. The handler checks the signature and the replay guard. Then it creates the order as digital_delivered with payment status paid. The same event sent twice creates one order.
  4. The system sends a signed download link by email or WhatsApp. The default is 72 hours and 5 downloads.
  5. The ledger books digital revenue to 4020 and the provider fee to 5060. The Meta Purchase event fires with an event id so it is counted once.
  6. A paid digital order counts as delivered revenue. A free bundled digital item adds GH₵0 and does not count. Digital orders stay out of the confirmation rate and the delivery success rate.

Stock Built in M0 (test), M4

Stock is counted at receipt, held per location and leaves once, at delivery.

Roles
Stock keeper, delivery agent, fulfilment lead, owner
Screens
Products, Inventory, Deliveries, agent app Stock page
Booked
Inventory purchase to 1200 (DR 1200, CR 1010); stock gap raises a Finding
  1. The stock keeper adds a shipment on Products: supplier, units shipped (300 from Accra Print House on 9 October), unit cost, shipping, customs and other costs.
  2. At the destination, for example the Logiswift warehouse in Tema, the keeper records the counted units: 288. The short quantity is 12. Landed cost per unit uses the counted quantity, not the shipped quantity.
  3. Stock lives in StockLevel per location and product. A transfer to a 3PL or an agent stays in transit until the receiver confirms. A smaller received count makes it disputed.
  4. When an order is delivered, stock leaves once. A returned order brings units back to a location. Stock may go negative at delivery. That raises a Finding and does not block the delivery.
  5. The owner counts against the 3PL dashboard on Inventory. The sample shows 100 in our records and 88 counted. The 12-unit gap opens a Finding worth GH₵362.50. The count also checks the 3PL's own returned count against ours.

Money and the delivery fee Built in M1, M5, M6, M7, M10

The delivery fee is booked once. The partner keeps it from cash. People are paid from one form.

Roles
Owner, finance clerk, call rep, delivery partner
Screens
Finance, Staff and roles, Settings (fee rules and rewards), agent app
Booked
5070 delivery fee once; 5050 and 2020 for rep commission; payout: DR 2020, CR cash
  1. Each partner has one fee rule in Settings: a fee per delivered, failed and returned order. The sample is Kofi at GH₵25 per delivered order. The fee is what we pay whoever delivers, a 3PL or an own agent.
  2. When the outcome happens, the fee is booked once to 5070. There is no second expense and no extra P&L line.
  3. The partner keeps the fee from the cash it collects and hands in the rest. Expected cash = cash collected minus fees. The 3PL payout and the agent deposit are matched to that net amount. The payout form never pays a fee.
  4. At delivery, a rep earns GH₵8 for an order they confirmed by call, plus 10% of the upsell value. Auto-confirmed orders earn no commission. Example: Adwoa has GH₵6,100 of extra value, so the upsell reward is GH₵610.
  5. At month close a job computes attainment on delivery-date numbers and creates reward entries. Cash tiers only. Call rep: 100% gives GH₵300, 120% gives GH₵500 and a paid day off. Delivery: 100% gives GH₵250, 120% gives GH₵400 and a paid day off. Companies and AI agents get no payout.
  6. Finance and Staff open the same payout form. It lists all earned and unpaid commission, upsell and reward entries for one person. Saving creates a payout, marks the entries paid and posts payable debit and cash credit.

Auto-confirm and the rep call queue Built in M7, M9

The message goes out at once. Only the rep call clock waits for opening hours.

Roles
Customer, call rep, call-centre manager
Screens
Rep screen (Today, Queue, My orders), Settings, Operations, Messages, Conversations
Booked
GH₵8 base commission only for an order a rep confirms by call
  1. A new order is pending confirmation. The auto-confirm message goes out at once by WhatsApp or SMS, also at night.
  2. A customer YES confirms the order. The order counts as auto-confirmed and closes the clock. It earns no commission.
  3. A customer NO does not cancel. The order goes to the call queue with a said NO flag. A rep calls once before any cancel. An order with no reply also waits in the queue.
  4. Time to first call is the time to the first rep call attempt. It counts only inside the operating hours that the owner enters in Settings, Operations. A night order starts its clock when the hours open. Target: 15 minutes. Alert: 30 minutes.
  5. The rep saves an outcome: confirmed, declined, wrong number or not reachable. A confirmed order that is later delivered earns GH₵8. The rep can add upsell lines. Reps can create orders and add or edit customers from the Queue.
  6. A rep can reply to customers on WhatsApp from the Conversations page.

Meta spend, campaign mapping, max CPA Built in M8

CodAdmin pulls Meta spend itself, maps campaigns to products and sets max CPA from the yield.

Roles
Owner, marketer
Screens
Connections, Marketing, Reports (Marketing tab)
Booked
DR 5080 ad spend, CR 1010; a re-pull that changes a day posts only the difference
  1. The owner connects the Meta ad account in Connections. The token is stored encrypted. Meta scopes and App Review are checked first (M8-T01).
  2. A job pulls today's spend every hour. A second job re-pulls the last 3 days once a day, at 05:00 Accra time, for the final figures. Spend stays in the ad account's currency, and the original amount is kept.
  3. The mapping screen links each campaign to a product, by name rule or by pick. An unmapped campaign shows Not mapped and raises a card. Its spend still counts in the totals.
  4. Spend converts to the store currency at the stored rate for that day. The spend posts as an expense: DR 5080, CR 1010.
  5. Max CPA = profit per delivered order before ads × order-to-cash yield. The copybook sample (store values) is GH₵185, which is ₦20,720. At 34% yield that gives ₦7,040. At 42% yield it gives ₦8,700. The build uses the weighted fee from the partners' fee rules.
  6. The Marketing page shows each campaign against its max CPA. Status is one of: Winning, On track, Over max CPA, Not mapped, Paused.

North Star setup Built in M1, M6

One yearly figure becomes quarter, month, role and person targets.

Roles
Owner
Screens
Setup wizard, Settings (North Star and targets), Targets, Leaderboard, Dashboard
Booked
Nothing. Targets drive pace, status and month-close rewards
  1. The setup wizard has six steps: store, first 3PL, annual North Star (GH₵1,800,000), driver targets, invite team, rewards link.
  2. The annual figure splits into quarters. Options: even each month, grow each quarter (360k, 420k, 480k, 540k), or custom. Quarters split into months. Months split into role and person targets.
  3. Three drivers set the volume. Planned AOV defaults to the average value of delivered orders in the last 30 days, and the owner can edit it. Confirmation rate is 70%. Delivery success rate is 60%.
  4. Example for October: GH₵180,000 ÷ GH₵249 = 723 delivered orders. Divided by 0.70 × 0.60, that is 1,721 orders. Divided by the 31 real days in the month, that is 56 orders a day.
  5. Max cost per order is computed, not typed. Reward tiers are not set here. The page shows a one-line link to Settings, Rewards.

Store setup and currencies Built in M1, M6

The owner picks a country and the store currency follows it. The owner can change the currency at any time. Rates come daily and the owner can override them.

Roles
Owner
Screens
Setup wizard step 1, Settings (Currencies and FX), Reports
Booked
Amounts in other currencies convert to the store currency at the day's rate
  1. In wizard step 1 the owner picks the country. The store currency is that country's currency and is set automatically. The region list also comes from the country.
  2. The store currency is not locked. The owner can change it in Settings at any time.
  3. Each record keeps its amount in the currency it was entered in. Screens and reports convert it to the store currency with the rate of the record's date. The original amount stays on the record.
  4. Other currencies are the ad account's currency (read from the Meta ad account) and supplier currencies.
  5. Rates arrive daily and automatically. The owner can set a manual rate. Every day keeps its rate in a history, and reports print the rate used that day. The stale-rate warning shows only for manual rates.

Refusal rule Built in M3 (counts), M7

Repeat refusers get a tag, then COD is switched off for them.

Roles
Delivery partner, call rep, owner
Screens
Customers, Orders, customer page, Dashboard card for repeat refusers
Booked
Nothing extra. A failed delivery books its fee as in Money
  1. Each failed delivery that the customer refused adds one to their refusal count.
  2. At 2 refusals the customer is marked High risk. Staff see a tag on the order and on the customer.
  3. At 3 refusals COD turns off for that customer. A new order from them goes to a manual call instead of the normal queue.
  4. The owner can override both states on the customer page. After an override the refusal count restarts at zero.
  5. A suggestion card lists customers with 2 or more refusals.

Assign by region (a Workflow) Built in M9

A Workflow sets the rule: when the region is X, assign to these agents. No coverage grid, no priority order and no fallback partner.

Roles
Owner, fulfilment lead
Screens
Workflows (Region condition, Assign to action), Orders
Booked
Nothing
  1. In Workflows, the owner builds a rule. Condition: Region is X. Action: Assign to one or more delivery agents or partners, split Evenly or Weighted.
  2. Many agents can serve one region. The region list comes from the store's country.
  3. When an order is ready to dispatch, the matching Workflow assigns it.
  4. If no Workflow matches, the order stays unassigned. The fulfilment lead assigns it.

AI read flow Built in M2, M11, M12

The AI answers from the same services as the Dashboard. A card click never saves data.

Roles
Owner, admin, manager (a drawer for call reps and agents is not in release one)
Screens
AI drawer on every admin screen, suggestion cards, Staff and roles (audit-log screen)
Booked
Nothing. An audit row is saved for every tool call
  1. A person opens the drawer from any screen. The page and its filters go with the question.
  2. The chat service asks the model. The tool list is the registry list for that person's permissions.
  3. The model calls read tools. Each call runs through the registry in the person's tenant and writes an audit row with channel chat. Phones and addresses are masked before data reaches the model.
  4. The answer streams back with a tool line. Every number comes from a tool result.
  5. Suggestion cards use the problem finders. A card button opens a screen with the form pre-filled. The person checks and confirms there. The click saves nothing.
  6. External agents use MCP with a key tied to an AI agent user and a role. From M12, write tools return a preview. A person confirms. The audit log keeps the before and after.
