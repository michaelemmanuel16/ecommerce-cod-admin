Fix first: 3 problems that affect every role

1 · "Delivery rate" means 3 different things

  • Dashboard and Reports: 26% = delivered ÷ all orders placed (376 ÷ 1,445).
  • Partner table: Logiswift 22% = delivered ÷ assigned, and that count includes orders still on the road.
  • Agent card: "Logiswift fails 55%" = failed ÷ closed orders.

The 3PL gets blamed for unconfirmed orders and for orders still on the road. Of the confirmed orders that have closed, 376 ÷ 778 = 48% were delivered. Use one name for each:

NameFormulaOwnerOct now
Confirmation rateconfirmed ÷ orders placedCall centre71%
Delivery success ratedelivered ÷ (delivered + failed + returned), closed orders onlyFulfilment48%
Order-to-cash yielddelivered ÷ orders placed, on order cohorts that have closedOwner, Marketer~34%

2 · The target maths does not add up

The North Star setup says that GH₵180k a month needs 60 orders a day, 70% confirmation and 40% delivery. Check: 60 × 30 × 70% × 40% × GH₵249 = GH₵125k. The plan falls short by GH₵55k before the month starts. The setup must calculate the missing driver from the others and show the result.

3 · The marketing target shows green while the business loses money

Marketing shows "CPA ₦5,900, under the ₦6,400 ceiling: on track", yet October shows a GH₵14.6k loss. The ceiling is a fixed number, but the affordable CPA (cost per order) depends on how many orders turn into cash:

Max CPA = profit per delivered order before ads × order-to-cash yield = (GH₵249 − 40 product − 34 fee = GH₵175 ≈ ₦19,600) × 34% ≈ ₦6,660 At 26% yield the max CPA falls to ≈ ₦5,100. At 40% it rises to ≈ ₦7,800.

Today's ₦6,400 ceiling is about right, but only if the yield stays near 34%. Let the ceiling move with the yield so the marketing target turns red when delivery drops.

Part of the October "loss" comes from timing. The month's ad spend is all booked, but 248 of its orders are still on the road or waiting to dispatch. The P&L by delivery date (your Q16 choice) stays as it is. Marketing also needs a cohort view: the orders placed in a period, judged once they close.
