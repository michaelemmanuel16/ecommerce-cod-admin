## Sniper (https://sniper.ng)

**Answer:** Sniper sells an all-in-one ecommerce dashboard with COD features, a 7-day free trial, and two monthly plans (N12,000 and N15,000). It has no public reviews I could find.

Findings:
- SEEN (https://sniper.ng/home): Headline "Run your entire ecom business from one dashboard: orders to cash to profit." Hero claim "Live in under 5 minutes. Profitable in your first week."
- SEEN (https://sniper.ng/home): Features: round-robin order assignment, stock with agents and low-stock alerts, delivery-agent cash tracking, "Duplicate Order Block" and "Fake Order" detection, income statement / cash flow / balance sheet, payroll schedules (PAYE, pension, NHF, NHIS), WhatsApp automation and chatbots, SMS/email automation, Paystack/Flutterwave/Stripe and Shopify/WooCommerce integrations.
- SEEN (https://sniper.ng/home): Price list (monthly): Basic N12,000 (5 products, 5 forms, 5 users); Big Boss N15,000 ("Most popular", unlimited products/forms/users); Enterprise N2,500,000 lifetime (own domain and server).
- SEEN (https://sniper.ng/home): "7 days free. No card required. Full access." Messaging and add-ons are charged separately.
- SEEN (https://sniper.ng/home): Targets "eCommerce brands, direct-response marketers and cash-on-delivery merchants." Uses naira and Nigerian providers. Testimonials mention Accra, Kano and Dakar, but no country list is given.
- SEEN (https://sniper.ng/home): Footer "© 2019 – 2026 Sniper." No founder named.
- SEEN (https://sniper.ng/home): Testimonial claims (customer-reported, unverified): "Conversion is up 31%"; one customer runs "8 products, 55+ staff, 4 warehouses, 10 store branches, over 48 delivery agents."
- SEEN (https://sniper.ng/signup login page, via https://sniper.ng/pricing fetch): Tagline "eCommerce dashboard for managing orders, inventory, marketing, operations & automations."
- UNVERIFIED: Dashboard numbers on the homepage (e.g., "Cash remitted N4.8M") look like illustrative screenshots.
