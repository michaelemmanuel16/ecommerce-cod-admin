What COD sellers use, and where CodAdmin stands

We studied 3 competitors (Sniper, SalesGee, SalesUnbox), the CodAdmin code, the open Linear issues and the E-Myth books. This page shows what we found. It is the input for the CodAdmin build plan.

17 Oct 2026 · Evidence: codadmin/docs/research/ · 177 screenshots · every key claim checked in fact-check.md

Bottom line
  • Sniper is the real benchmark. It covers the full cash-on-delivery loop and adds strong finance and fraud controls. It costs ₦12,000–₦15,000 a month.
  • No competitor connects to couriers, and no competitor has a working AI agent. Sniper shows an "AI Sales Rep" in its menu, but it is not built. These are the two clear gaps CodAdmin can take.
  • CodAdmin already has most of the core loop. Orders, forms, Paystack, agent stock, agent cash collection and accounting all exist.
  • But CodAdmin is not safe to sell yet. Unpaid tenants can use it, the accounts are not split per business, and 12 data tables are not split per business.
  • Finished work is stuck on branches. The order state machine and multi-store were built but never merged.
  • Nothing in CodAdmin covers goals, targets, ad spend or team accountability. These are the E-Myth areas the owner asked for.

The competitors

Three tools, three different shapes

Sniper sniper.ng

Basic ₦12,000/mo · Big Boss ₦15,000/mo · Enterprise ₦2.5M once
What it is
A COD operations app. 152 menu items. Built with Lovable and Supabase.
Strongest
Agent cash float flagged after 48 hours, remittance matched to the bank, full statements, payroll, 7 fraud controls, 9 roles that can hide profit and customer data.
Weak
No courier link. AI Sales Rep not built. Ad ROI is split by spend share, so it is an estimate. Very dense screens.

SalesGee getsalesgee.com

Scale ₦12,500/mo · Platinum ₦20,000/mo · Enterprise ₦1.5M/yr · homepage also advertises a free plan
What it is
A WordPress plugin with a React screen. Orders, reps, agent stock by waybill, staff targets.
Strongest
Simple order flow with 13 call-centre statuses, staff targets and a leaderboard, form editor with live preview.
Weak
Remittance is typed in by hand per order. Only one fraud check (same-day duplicate). No AI. No page links (no back button).

SalesUnbox salesunbox.africa

Each module ₦30k / ₦60k / ₦150k per month · Store ₦5k / ₦12k per month
What it is
A general suite for Nigerian SMEs: inventory, CRM, accounting, online store. Says it has more than 5,000 businesses.
Strongest
Real double-entry accounting and multi-location stock.
Weak
No COD flow, no call centre, no agent cash, no fraud checks. Not a direct competitor.

No public reviews or complaints exist for any of the three. Sniper and SalesGee have very small social followings.

Numbers worth tracking

KPIs that fit a COD business

Sniper uses "realised" numbers: money counts only when the order is delivered and the cash is collected. These are the most useful ones we found.

Money

  • Realised revenue: delivered and settled orders only
  • Cash in the field: delivered, not yet paid in
  • Revenue leak: unremitted cash, returns, cancelled after confirmation
  • Contribution margin after delivery fees and returns

Marketing and sales

  • Cost per delivered order (ad spend ÷ delivered orders)
  • Realised ROAS (realised revenue ÷ ad spend)
  • Confirmation rate per rep
  • Customer trust score: delivered ÷ all orders for that phone

Delivery

  • Delivery rate per agent, product and state
  • Return rate per agent
  • Days from dispatch to delivery
  • Failed delivery reasons

Team

  • Staff cost per order
  • Performance against target
  • Orders handled and delivered per staff member

Note: CodAdmin's delivery rate today divides orders delivered in the period by orders created in the period. SalesGee makes the same mistake. The rebuild must use one formula everywhere.

CodAdmin today

What must be fixed before anything new

  • high
    Unpaid tenants can use the app. No check blocks suspended or unpaid businesses. Order and user limits are only displayed.
  • high
    Accounts are not split per business. Account codes are unique across the whole app, and new businesses get no chart of accounts.
  • high
    12 tables have no business ID. For example agent cash collections, deposits, payouts and calls.
  • high
    The repo and the live database do not match. 3 migrations are empty placeholders. The changes were made by hand.
  • medium
    Money is stored two ways. Orders use decimal numbers that can round; the ledger uses exact numbers.
  • medium
    Revenue has 4 formulas on different screens.
  • medium
    Finished work sits on branches. The order state machine is on a branch on the Mac only, 5 months old and never pushed. Multi-store is on 3 GitHub branches, 3 months old and not merged.
  • medium
    Workflows skip the rules. Two workflow actions change orders directly, so stock and ledger entries are not made.
E-Myth

How the book maps to the app

Gerber's model: a business is a system that runs without the owner. Marketing makes leads, sales converts them, operations fulfils them, and finance supports all three. Every role gets a written contract before anyone is hired. Every system gets its own numbers.

Corrections and limits

What we fixed and what we could not see

  • A first search said Sniper prices were in US dollars. That came from old page code. The live prices are in naira.
  • The code audit said the MCP server has 13 tools. It has 14.
  • SalesGee's homepage advertises a free plan, but the app's own plan list has no free plan.
  • Not seen: staff and driver logins at Sniper, SalesGee's real server (the demo is offline mock data), and the SalesUnbox app behind its login.
  • Nothing was submitted, saved or deleted on any competitor site.
